Scrum Beyond IT · Free Practical Tools

Three Free Tools for Monday Morning

Built for teams outside software development. Zero cookies, zero logins, zero trackers. Everything runs locally in your browser in English, German, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Tool 1 · 50-Question Scenario Pool

Scrum Exam Traps Quiz

Test your instincts on the classic PSM I and CSPO traps without software jargon. Every run draws fresh scenarios and explains why non-IT teams stumble over each rule in practice.

  • 50 scenario questions across 4 focused decks or full exam simulation
  • Instant plain-language breakdown after every answer
  • One-click replay of only the questions you missed
Start Exam Traps Quiz
Tool 2 · Sprint Retrospective Facilitator

Beyond-IT Retrospective Card Deck

Stop repeating "what went well, what went badly" until nobody speaks. Pick what your team actually needs to fix, draw a structured retro format, and capture 1 or 2 real actions.

  • Four practical focus modes: bottlenecks, meetings, handoffs, and energy
  • Built-in phase timer and concrete non-IT guiding questions
  • One-click plain-text summary or printable card for the team wall
Open Retro Card Deck
Tool 3 · Physical & Remote Daily

15-Minute Daily Timer "At the Wall"

Keep your Daily at 15 minutes whether your team stands in front of a whiteboard or shares a screen in a video call. Green and red buffer signals keep uneven speaking times fair.

  • Switch between Whiteboard Fullscreen and compact Remote Screen-Share Bar
  • Green/Red pacing buffer with "Next Person" tap and After-Daily parking lot
  • Optional soft 2-stage chime and shareable ?wall= link for team chat
Launch Daily Timer
