Tool 3 · Physical & Remote Daily

15-Minute Daily Timer "At the Wall"

Keep your Daily at 15 minutes at a physical whiteboard or in a remote video call. Short updates donate time to real blockers, shown clearly by the Green/Red buffer.

15:00
Green buffer: ~2:30 min left per remaining person On Track

02Who needs help today?

Why 15 minutes changes the whole room

In The Number on the Wall, the morning stand-up stops being a polite status show the moment the team puts the real bottleneck in sight and moves deep debates to After-Daily.
